One 1024×1024 newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Thursday
19 November 2026 — v5: the v4 structure and verified 42-story set, with a
drawn scene illustration on every one of the 15 main stories (a Shenzhen
skyline flying the US, Chinese and Russian flags; the US Capitol; the
Brandenburg Gate; Gaza tanks; a Vice City night skyline for GTA VI;
Voyager 1 in a starfield; Odesa port cranes under fire; Cyclone Akvan's
spiral; the Peace Palace with the scales of justice; SAFF stadium
floodlights; a Borno acacia and army truck; Indian solar fields; a
Pew skyline of church, mosque and temple; Doha's U-17 stadium; and a
refugee boat at sea). Plus the region tally bar, the "Day in UTC"
timeline strip, a By The Numbers strip, a filled-silhouette "Where It
Happened" map with a numbered pin for every main story, and 27 one-line
stories under "Also on Thursday". A DATE CHECK strip names the big
stories deliberately left out because they broke before today. Every
story was verified against the wire and newspaper sources named on the
page; all artwork is original, drawn in code with PIL.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte
(`draw.py` is the build tool that drew it; it is not run at entry time).
